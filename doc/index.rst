Welcome to Orange3 Neural Viz documentation!
============================================

Widgets for building, training and watching small neural networks in `Orange <https://orangedatamining.com>`_, in the spirit of the
`TensorFlow Playground <https://playground.tensorflow.org/>`_, but working on your own data.

Install the add-on from Orange's *Add-ons* dialog (search for ``orange3-neuralviz``) or with ``pip install orange3-neuralviz``.
See the `README <https://github.com/chrislee35/orange3-neuralviz#readme>`_ for details.
